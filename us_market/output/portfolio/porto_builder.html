How to Use
▣Checkbox (orange/green block, left) — Click to select or deselect a stock. Allocation % recalculates automatically across all selected stocks in real-time.
⬡Ticker Name — Click to open the full Sniper Detail dashboard for that ticker: backtest history, signal table, edge & quality breakdown.
▤TF Dropdown — Switch time-frame per card individually (5D / 10D / 20D). EV updates immediately on the card and in the footer basket.
▩EV / Alloc box (right block) — Click to open the Stress Test popup. Shows Win Rate, Loss Rate, Avg Win, Avg Loss, EV and Avg Return for each time-frame.
▬Sticky Footer — Displays Average EV of your basket, count of selected stocks, and a scrollable chip list showing each ticker with its score-weighted allocation %. All values update live.
