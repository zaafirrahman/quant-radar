About This System

Quant Radar is a research-driven market intelligence system designed to identify high-momentum assets and validate their statistical edge through event-based backtesting.

The system operates in two layers: a Radar Engine that detects abnormal momentum and flow activity, and a Sniper Engine that evaluates the reliability of these signals using historical performance metrics.

Built for systematic analysis, this platform aims to bridge the gap between market scanning and data-driven decision making.
